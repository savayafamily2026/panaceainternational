PanaceaApproach

Four stages, in writing. You can stop after any of them.

  1. 01

    Diagnostic

    Free. We learn how the company runs and where it stalls.

  2. 02

    Scope & terms

    Scope, fee and deliverables in writing, with success measures for the first 90 days.

  3. 03

    Transition

    Work moves over from current providers. Each handover is confirmed in writing.

  4. 04

    Operate & review

    We run the work and review it every quarter.

Principles

  • Only qualified hands

    Licensed work goes to licensed professionals. We disclose their credentials.

  • One point of accountability

    One team owns the outcome. No handoffs between vendors.

  • Conflicts disclosed

    We sometimes advise and invest in the same company. We disclose it in writing first.

  • No lock-in

    Every service stands alone. Retainers end on 30 days’ notice, with handover.
