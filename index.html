Open roles

AI Engineer

Build and maintain the AI systems that automate operational, accounting, legal and IT work for our clients, from model integration and retrieval to evaluation and production infrastructure.

  • 3+ years of software engineering experience
  • Strong Python or TypeScript
  • Experience shipping LLM-based applications to production

Forward Deployed Engineer

Work directly with client teams to scope, configure and roll out our systems inside their operations. You own each deployment from first workshop to stable handover.

  • Engineering background with client-facing experience
  • Comfortable working across data, integrations and workflows
  • Clear written and spoken communication

Client Support Associate

Handle day-to-day client requests, onboarding and document management, and coordinate with our specialist teams so each request is resolved quickly and correctly.

  • 2+ years in client service, operations or administration
  • Organised, accurate and responsive
  • Familiarity with accounting, HR or immigration processes is a plus

Operations & Administration Associate

Support the internal running of the company: bookkeeping support, vendor management, scheduling and entity administration.

  • 2+ years in operations or office administration
  • Working knowledge of bookkeeping tools
  • High attention to detail
